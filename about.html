About & Contact

"Iris Two Eyes" is an independent creative project: an AI character, Iris, and her interactive series. The project is run by one person, her creator.

What is AI here?

Iris's images, animation and voice are generated with AI tools. The scripts are written by her creator. Every episode carries the label "AI-generated content".

Why this website

It presents the series and hosts the legal pages of the "Iris Two Eyes" application, which publishes the episodes to Iris's own TikTok account.
