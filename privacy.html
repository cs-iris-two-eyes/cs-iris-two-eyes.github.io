Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Who uses this app

"Iris Two Eyes" is used only by the creator of the AI character Iris, to publish videos to Iris's own TikTok account. No other person signs in to the app.

2. Data we access

When Iris's TikTok account authorizes the app, TikTok provides an access token, the account's open ID and basic profile information (display name and avatar). These are used only to publish videos to that same account and to check the publishing status.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store or process any personal data of TikTok viewers or other users. We do not sell or share any data. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and this website sets no cookies.

4. Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored privately on the creator's own computer, are never published, and are deleted when the app's authorization is revoked.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings, under "Security" then "Manage app permissions".
